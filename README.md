### Game Details & Guide
Introduction to Tron


Tron is a classic browser game that has been entertaining players for decades. Developed by Disney, the game follows the story of Kevin Flynn, who crash-lands on the digital world of Grid. The objective of the game is to navigate through levels and collect treasures while avoiding hazards.


How to Play


To play Tron, you will need a standard web browser. Here's how to start:
• Open your browser and type 
Tron Online

• Click on the "Play Now" button to begin.
• You will be transported into the digital world of Grid, where you'll find yourself in a futuristic environment.

The game is divided into different levels, each with its own unique challenges and obstacles. To progress through the levels, you'll need to navigate your character, known as Kevin Flynn, through various areas.


Game Controls

• Use the arrow keys to move Kevin's avatar left or right.
• Use the space bar to jump.
• Use the shift key to break through solid walls (requires skill and practice).
• To change the difficulty level, use the F12 key.

The game also features a variety of power-ups, such as the "Motherboard" which allows you to speed up your progress and the "Glitch" which gives you temporary invincibility.


Objective


The ultimate goal is to reach the final level, where you'll find the treasure hidden deep within Grid. Along the way, you'll need to collect resources, avoid hazards, and solve puzzles to unlock new areas.


Tips and Tricks for Success
